Research-active teaching

Meet the lecturers.

The programme is developed and taught by researchers whose expertise spans metaphysics, epistemology, philosophy of language, philosophy of mind and philosophy of science.

A programme shaped by current research.

Specialisation courses are designed around the lecturers' active research, giving students direct access to contemporary debates and internationally connected philosophical scholarship.

Across the core fields

Together, the team covers the programme's three principal areas—metaphysics, epistemology and philosophy of language—alongside philosophy of mind and philosophy of science.

Anna-Sofia Maurin

Professor of Theoretical Philosophy · Programme coordinator

Anna-Sofia Maurin works primarily in metaphysics and metametaphysics. Her research includes tropes, unity in complexity, the epistemology of metaphysics, regress arguments, metaphysical explanation, social ontology and non-causal explanation.

Daniel Giberman

Senior Lecturer in Theoretical Philosophy

Daniel Giberman specialises in metaphysics, with research on concrete particulars, properties, persistence, mereology, modality and related questions about the fundamental structure of reality.

Ylwa Sjölin Wirling

Senior Lecturer in Theoretical Philosophy

Ylwa Sjölin Wirling's main research interests are epistemology, philosophy of science and metaphilosophy, with additional work touching on metaphysics and value theory.

Kevin Reuter

Senior Lecturer in Theoretical Philosophy

Kevin Reuter works at the intersection of philosophy of mind, language and science, experimental philosophy, epistemology and conceptual engineering. His research combines philosophical analysis with empirical and corpus-linguistic methods.
